# Overview
Comprehensive Excel Sales & Financial Performance reports analyzing global revenue streams, product division growth, and P&L margin trends across 23 international markets for AtliQ Hardwares.
This repository contains a comprehensive Sales & Finance Analytics project completed for AtliQ Hardwares, a leading consumer electronics and hardware manufacturer operating globally across B2B, B2C, and e-commerce distribution channels.

# Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze multi-year enterprise datasets to empower decision-makers with actionable insights into revenue growth, customer performance, target variances, and market-by-market profitability (P&L).

# Technical Stack & Tools Used
**Business Intelligence Tool**: Microsoft Excel

**Data Transformation & Cleaning**: Power Query (ETL process, unpivoting datasets, custom column transformations)
**Data Analytics & Modeling**: Pivot Tables, Dynamic Named Ranges, Data Model Relationship Creation
**Key Formulas & Functions**: DAX, Power query, Power Pivot, Structured Table References,

# Key Analytics Reports Covered
## 1. Sales Analytics Reports

(a) **Customer Net Sales Performance**: Tracks multi-year net sales (FY2019–FY2021) for global retail and e-commerce customers (e.g., Amazon, AtliQ e Store, Walmart, Flipkart) to assess customer growth and account expansion.

(b) **Market Performance vs. Target**: Evaluates actual revenue against pre-defined targets across 23 global markets, highlighting variance amounts and percentage deviations.

(c) **Product & Division Level Performance**: Analyzes revenue and volume breakdown across product divisions (P&A, PC, and N&S), along with SKU-level rankings:

 * Top 10 Products by Revenue Growth
 * Top 5 & Bottom 5 Products by Sales Volume
 * Performance Evaluation of New Product Launches in FY2021

 * Top 5 Revenue-Generating Countries in FY2021
## 2. Finance Analytics Reports
(a) **Country-Wise P&L Statement**: Multi-year Profit & Loss (P&L) statement breakdown detailing Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Margin, and Gross Margin % per market.

(b) **Quarterly Gross Margin Breakdown**: Evaluates seasonal Gross Margin % (GM%) variations across global sub-zones (ANZ, India, NA, NE, ROA, SE) across quarters Q1–Q4 for FY2019–FY2021.

(c) **Market-by-Market P&L (FY2021)**: A consolidated financial view showing total revenue, cost structure, and net profit margins across all active operating countries.

# Key Insights & Strategic Findings

(1) **Overall Revenue Trajectory**: Net Sales grew substantially from $87.5M in FY2019 to $598.9M in FY2021 (+304.5% overall growth).

(2) **Top Customers & E-Commerce Impact**: Key enterprise accounts like Amazon ($82.1M in FY21) and direct-to-consumer channels like AtliQ e Store ($53.0M in FY21) served as primary growth catalysts.

(3) **Target Variance**: Total Net Sales reached $598.9M against an ambitious global target of $653.8M (-8.4% variance). Core markets like India ($161.3M) and the USA ($87.8M) delivered the highest revenue absolute values.

(4) **Profitability Trends**: Total Gross Margin expanded from $36.2M (41.4%) in 2019 to $218.2M (36.4%) in 2021. High GM% performance was led by markets such as New Zealand (48.2%), Japan (46.5%), and the UK (45.1%).

(5) **Division Performance**: The Peripherals & Accessories (P&A) division led product performance ($338.4M in FY21), while FY2021 new product launches contributed $176.2M in added top-line sales.


# Business Recommendations
* **Optimize Discount Strategies:** Review trade discounts and promotional allowances for key retail partners (e.g., Amazon, Croma) to stop gross margin erosion.
* **Improve Market Demand Forecasting:** Refine target-setting algorithms in markets like India and Poland to minimize variance between projected and actual sales.
* **Product Portfolio Management:** Evaluate bottom-performing SKUs for potential bundling or phased phase-outs to reduce carrying costs and improve product mix profitability.
* **Regional Supply Chain Cost Control:** Focus on COGS reduction strategies in sub-zones showing sharp GM% declines (ANZ and India).
